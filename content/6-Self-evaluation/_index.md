### Strengths

**Independent learning and knowledge acquisition**  
Because most of the program is based on self-study at home, I have practiced reading workshops and technical documentation and following instructional videos before turning what I learned into a lab or worklog entry. I can approach new groups of AWS services and gradually connect them into a more complete architecture.

**Proactiveness during hands-on work**  
I proactively prepare the environment, follow configuration steps, validate results, and clean up resources after labs. In the project, I have studied the repository and analyzed its Dockerfile, Docker Compose configuration, Gemini settings, and persistence mechanism based on ChromaDB, LanceDB, and SQLite.

**Ability to organize technical knowledge**  
Maintaining weekly worklogs helps me organize objectives, tasks, references, and results. I now have a clearer understanding of the relationships among compute, storage, networking, databases, security, and monitoring instead of treating each service as an isolated topic.

**Collaboration and openness to feedback**  
During in-person sessions and project discussions, I communicate with mentors and team members to agree on an approach. I am willing to revise content or technical decisions when I receive relevant feedback.

### Areas for Improvement

**Hands-on cloud deployment experience**  
I have gained a foundation through labs and architecture design, but my experience deploying a complete application on AWS remains limited. The RAG project is still running locally and being prepared for deployment, so I need additional practice with ECR, ECS Fargate, EFS, ALB, IAM, SSM Parameter Store, and CloudWatch Logs during the remaining weeks.

**Systematic troubleshooting**  
When I encounter an issue in an unfamiliar area, I sometimes need additional time to identify its cause. I need to improve my troubleshooting sequence by checking the original requirement, configuration, credentials, IAM permissions, network path, logs, resource status, and actual result. Recording hypotheses and each validation step will help me avoid unfocused trial and error.

**Time management and focus**  
A flexible self-study format can lead to an overly broad learning scope. I need to break work into small deliverables, assign a deadline to each part, and complete the main objective before researching additional topics.

**Technical communication**  
I need to describe issues more clearly and concisely by including the objective, environment, reproduction steps, expected behavior, actual behavior, relevant logs, and previous attempts. This structure will allow mentors and team members to provide more effective support.

**Testing and delivery quality**  
I need to continue improving how I design test cases, negative tests, health checks, and acceptance criteria. For the RAG project, this includes validating PDF upload, indexing, retrieval, citations, persistent data, secret management, and recovery after a container restart.

### Improvement Plan

* Complete the project deployment and testing tasks planned for Weeks 6–8 without reporting a task as complete before evidence is available.
* Use a checklist for each lab and project task, including preparation, implementation, testing, evidence collection, and cleanup.
* Plan self-study activities by week, prioritize required deliverables, and limit time spent on out-of-scope topics.
* Record logs, errors, root causes, and resolutions to build reusable troubleshooting experience.
* Practice reporting progress and describing technical issues using a consistent structure.
* Continue studying AWS security, monitoring, cost optimization, and system operations.

> **Conclusion:** At the current stage, the internship has helped me establish an AWS foundation, improve my independent learning skills, and better understand the process of moving an application from a local environment to a cloud architecture. I recognize that I still need more experience in hands-on deployment, testing, and troubleshooting. Therefore, an overall rating of Good appropriately reflects both my current progress and the skills I need to continue developing during the remaining internship period.
